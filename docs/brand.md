# Helios mark

**Meridian** is the selected mark. The outer circle is the world. Two curved meridians form an H, while the horizontal path ends at a warm point. That point means a sourced place to help, not a completed registration. The logo stays quiet beside the photographic globe.

- `public/brand/helios-mark.svg`: warm mark for the navy header and dark surfaces.
- `public/brand/helios-mark-on-light.svg`: navy mark for light surfaces.
- `public/brand/helios-favicon.svg`: selected mark on navy for browser tabs and app icons.
- `public/brand/helios-concepts.svg`: the three-direction study, including the unselected Horizon and Passage concepts.

Keep the mark clear at 32 px and above when possible. Use the favicon asset below that size. Do not place the transparent warm mark directly on cream or a busy photograph. The organization logos on the globe retain their own identity; Helios is the frame around them.
