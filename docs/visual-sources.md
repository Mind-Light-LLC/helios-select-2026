# Globe imagery and organization marks

Checked 2026-10-03. The globe uses NASA GIBS Blue Marble, August 2004, as a photographic basemap. It is historical imagery, not a live Earth view. The [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) permit factual uses with source acknowledgement and prohibit implying NASA endorsement. The map links to [NASA GIBS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/global-imagery-browse-services) beside the imagery.

Three organization marks are shown for identification. Each file came from the organization's own website:

The other records use neutral initials. [Australian Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org.au/about/the-red-cross-emblem/guide-to-emblem-use/) restricts emblem use, and [TECHO Chile](https://cl.techo.org/terminos-y-condiciones-de-uso/) prohibits unauthorized duplication of its marks. Do not replace initials with their emblems or logos without a reviewed right to display them. Neither the imagery nor the marks indicate organizational endorsement of Helios.
