## Donation evidence

[Project Open Hand's official donation form](https://www.openhand.org/donate) publishes a $10 minimum for a custom gift. The other five official donation paths in the demo do not have a verified $10 minimum, so Helios asks the person to check the organization's form. A Helios link never means a payment was made.
